Our Mission

The Aims

The goal of “Project Roshni” is to provide educational opportunities for deserving students from low-income families in Pakistan. By offering financial and academic support, we aim to empower these children, enabling them to continue their education and achieve their full potential. This initiative aligns with the mission to create a positive social impact and promote access to quality education for all.

How to Qualify

Requirements

To qualify for the support, applicants must meet the following criteria:

  1. 01

    Academic Record

    A copy of the applicant’s academic record for the past two years. Eligible students must be in grade 8 or onwards, studying towards O Level or the Agha Khan Board.

  2. 02

    Financial Background Information

    Verification that the applicant comes from a low-income family, defined as a household with less than 90,000 rupees in household income. Evidence can be in the form of payslips, bank statements, or other applicable documents.

  3. 03

    Two Written Answers

    • Question 1 (500 words) — Tell us more about yourself, why you deserve this scholarship, and how it will help you achieve your goals. Please include information about your extracurricular activities and future ambitions.
    • Question 2 (300 words) — Tell us about something that interests you and why. Your answer does not need to be related to this scholarship — it is a chance for us to learn more about you and your interests.
All three components — academic record, financial background, and written answers — are reviewed together, giving every applicant a fair and complete assessment.

How We Raise Support

Fundraising Initiatives

1. Contributions from Society Events

To support “Project Roshni,” we have partnered with two student-run societies at the University: the Warwick Pakistani Society and Warwick UNICEF on Campus. These societies contribute to the cause through their event profits and special stalls throughout the academic year.

2. Online Marketing and Network

The @projectroshnipk Instagram page markets the project, and the network of students involved helps us connect with donors who support the initiative by sponsoring one child each.

Every partnership begins with a single sponsorship — society events and personal networks turn everyday fundraising into individual scholarships.

Telling Our Story

Marketing the Project

The marketing approach highlights the initiative’s benefits, such as providing valuable opportunities and resources to students. “Project Roshni” is marketed across multiple platforms to ensure maximum visibility and engagement.

Social Media Promotions

The Instagram page of the project shares updates on fundraising events and student progress, with regular posts and stories highlighting students’ grades, their life stories and ECA achievements.

Student Feedback and Testimonials

At the end of the academic year, we showcase feedback from students through the annual report they are required to submit, highlighting their achievements in academics and extracurricular activities alongside their suggestions for improvements. This helps illustrate the project’s impact and attract further support.

Consistent storytelling from social media updates to annual reports turns everyday supporters into long-term partners for our students.

What Sponsorship Covers

Supporting Our Students

Financial Support

This covers the monthly school fees of the students and provides financial support for their approved extracurricular activities.

Academic Support

  • The initiative organises workshops throughout the year on topics such as journalism and the art of public speaking, which require students to complete relevant coursework.
  • Additional academic support is available on request, contingent on donor approval, ensuring every student receives support tailored to their needs.
